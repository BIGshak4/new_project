# Muxi's wardrobe

Mode: Operate. Precisely specified extension of Mineral, with existing dark Ion tokens.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friendly logic-component character is the user's visible identity; Muxi helps them dress it with earned coins.
OWN-WORLD: Mineral semantic UI variables, Heebo, thin outlines and flat shop surfaces. Character color comes from a finite canonical palette. Logical silhouettes, cheerful faces, short limbs and an unmistakable Job_Run shirt stay legible in every skin.
STORY: Muxi welcomes the player from behind a real shop counter and helps them browse shirts. Four generic skins cost 20 coins each; six electronics/programming meme shirts cost 40. Inspect the large print, buy once, equip it, then customize the free component and color. New members may choose their free character or skip during first use.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A striped canopy, Muxi behind the counter, playful speech and a featured printed shirt form a friendly booth. The top bar contains games, shop and the saved avatar. The collection and free editor follow. On phones the booth and shelf stack without hiding purchase status or actions.
CHARACTERS: Rounded component-specific bodies, a broad neck seated behind the collar, expressive faces and short articulated limbs. Compact avatars use clear print identifiers; full jokes open at reading size in the duel. Two duel avatars face inward without mirroring their lettering. Body edges remain visible on dark Ion surfaces. Job_Run appears as a small hem brand on printed shirts.
FORM: Extend the established product. Native vector characters support runtime component/color/skin selection. No new palette or concept seed; no purchased advantage, no cash payments, no AI during play.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Persistence and trust

Server stores user-owned config and validates purchases under the same coin lock as duels. Public preview uses explicitly simulated state only. Do not pretend a failed save or purchase succeeded. Anonymous clients cannot write inventory or choose arbitrary prices. User authorized deployment on 29 October 2026.
