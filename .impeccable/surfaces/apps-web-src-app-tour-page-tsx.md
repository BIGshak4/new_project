# Muxi: learn by trying

Mode: Operate. A local extension of the approved adaptive Ion product, not a new visual world.

## User request, 2026-10-18

Two optional, replayable, hands-on tours: a brief introduction and a genuinely comprehensive tour covering a consolidated subject overview, code, truth tables, circuit editing/simulation, hints, images, feedback, interviews and progress. Recommend the comprehensive tour. Make choosing, skipping and exiting obvious. The temporary guide is Muxi / מוקסי, a cute multiplexer with eyes and short pointing arms/legs. Test each option and both entire tours, repairing failures.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A friendly little multiplexer points out one useful action at a time, and the learner tries it in the real editors.
OWN-WORLD: Inherit Ion cream/plum and midnight/lilac/amber tokens, Heebo and technical monospace, 7px controls, 12px working panels and thin dividers. Native elongated 4:1 MUX silhouette: four data input lines at left, two select lines below, one output at right; cute face and short limbs, no MUX lettering. Finite, reduced-motion-aware poses. No replacement of the current product design.
STORY: Choose full (39 stations, recommended) or short (10); try a clearly described action; continue, skip a station, jump chapters, pause, resume or exit; reopen from the permanent workspace launcher or the public /tour route.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Welcoming Muxi beside a short introduction. Two adjacent choices on desktop, stacked on phones. Full tour has a written recommendation; skip/exit is visible in the header and below the choices. During a tour a pointing coach sits beside the exercise, with a concrete task, labelled progress and honest sample-data context. A second small Muxi and arrow sit beside the exact actionable control. Each intermediate click gets its own target and automatic scrolling. Phone navigation remains available while the target is in view.
FORM: A fixed introduction workspace keeps authenticated work mounted underneath. Reuse production AnswerEditor, CircuitEditor and CircuitTruthTable; locally authored examples for workflows that would otherwise mutate account data. No backend, schema, model or storage changes. Example feedback is explicitly fixed instructional material, not an assessment of typed answers. Show an authored 85/100 example score with its own fixed sample answer and rubric. Typed responses receive neutral ungraded receipts; follow-up reference is hidden until explicitly opened.
FINISH: Bounded desktop/mobile, HE/EN and light/dark evidence; real browser traversal of both tours; independent finish review and extension documentation.

## Verification boundaries

Local state and per-user, versioned station checkpoints only; never persist answers/images in tour progress, call a model, or add XP. Theme and language controls are genuine product preferences. Preserve keyboard access to CodeMirror and nested dialog scroll locks. Native expanded circuit dialogs must retain usable coaching. Keep both the original product and the tour available with reduced motion and when storage is unavailable.

## User correction, 2026-10-18

The supplied screenshots override the previous release review. Collapse eight repetitive per-topic question stations into one subject overview in both tours. Guide actual entry points and intermediate controls (open editor, copy code, language selection, circuit component/input toggle, simulation, truth-table generation) and scroll them into view automatically. Always separate task completion from correctness. Preserve the existing Ion themes, optional replay/skip/exit, real editor reuse and account-isolated examples.
