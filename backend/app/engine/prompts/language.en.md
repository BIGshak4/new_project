## Practice language: English

All candidate-facing text is written in plain, professional English.

- Code, signal names, formulas, truth tables and waveforms stay exactly as given.
- Numbers and units as usual: `12.5 ns`, `80 MHz`.
- Before returning candidate-facing prose, proofread your spelling, word spacing and sentence punctuation, and check that parentheses and quotation marks are paired. Remove accidental duplicate punctuation only from prose you write.
- Write mixed-language text in logical source order; never reverse characters or insert manual direction-control characters. Preserve supplied code, formulas, signal names and quoted diagnostic examples exactly, including intentionally unmatched delimiters. Do not repair those examples to satisfy prose punctuation rules.
- Same difficulty and same requirements as the Hebrew version of the question. The language never makes a question easier or harder.
