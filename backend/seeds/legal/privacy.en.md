# JobRun privacy policy

Version {version}

This document explains what information JobRun collects about you, why, who receives it and what your rights are. It describes what the site actually does.

## 1. Who is responsible for the information

The site is operated by {operator}. For any question or request about privacy, write to {contact_email}.

## 2. What we collect

**Account.** Your e-mail address, username and the avatar you chose. Your password is handled by the sign-in service (Supabase); it is stored encrypted and we do not see it.

**Your goal.** The job types you are preparing for, your career stage, how many minutes a day you want to practise, and your interview date if you chose to give it.

**Practice.** The answers you send (text, code, circuit drawings and photos you attached), the hints you asked for, whether you opened the solution, how confident you felt before a question if you said so, and when you started and sent each answer.

**Evaluations.** What the AI system decided about each answer (strong, partial or needs work), the feedback written for you, an impression of how you solved it (for example, whether you explained your reasoning), and your level in each topic over time.

**Mock interviews, games and friends.** The questions and answers of a mock interview and its report, duel and game results, your coin balance, your friends list, and when you were last active.

**Reports and requests.** A report that you saw a question at a certain company, a report about a question, and support requests with the files you attached.

**Technical information.** A record of requests to the server (time, action and account identifier), the general type of device, the IP address as the hosting providers record it, and what each use of the AI cost.

**In your browser.** The site keeps your sign-in, language, colour theme and answer drafts in the browser. The site has no third-party advertising or tracking tools.

**Voice dictation.** If you turn on dictation, your browser turns speech into text and may send the recording to its own speech provider. We receive only the text.

## 3. Why we use the information

- To run your account and the practice.
- **To adapt the preparation to you personally.** From your answers and evaluations the site chooses which questions to offer, at what difficulty, what to practise each day and what feedback to give. This is the consent you give when you sign up.
- To check and improve the questions and the quality of the evaluation. For this, members of the team may read answers and evaluations.
- To protect the site: preventing abuse, usage limits and finding faults.

We do not sell information, we do not pass it to employers or advertisers, and we do not use your answers to train AI models.

## 4. What is required and what is optional

You have no legal duty to give us information. Opening an account needs an e-mail address, a password, a username and the consent to personalisation; personalisation is the service itself, and without the consent we cannot open an account. The job type, career stage and practice time are needed to build a plan. The interview date, photos, dictation, friends, company reports and files in support requests are optional.

Do not put an employer's confidential information, material covered by a non-disclosure agreement or other people's personal details in your answers.

## 5. Who receives the information

Providers that process information for us:

- **Supabase:** the database, sign-in and file storage.
- **DigitalOcean:** the server that runs the site.
- **OpenAI:** checking the answers and writing the feedback. The question, your answer and photos you attached are sent. Your name, e-mail address and account identifier are not.
- **Vercel:** running code you submitted, in an isolated environment. The code and the account identifier are sent.

Other users: friends see your username, your avatar and whether you are online, and a duel opponent sees the result of the duel between you. They do not see your practice answers or your levels.

We give information to an authority only when the law requires us to.

## 6. Processing outside Israel

The providers in section 5 store and process the information on servers outside Israel, in Europe and the United States.

## 7. How long the information is kept

The information is kept for as long as the account exists. If you ask to close the account we delete your personal information, except what the law requires us to keep. Backup copies are deleted later.

## 8. Your rights

You may see the information kept about you, ask to correct wrong information and ask to delete information. To do so, write to {contact_email}. You can withdraw your consent at any time by asking to close the account. If you did not get a satisfactory answer, you can turn to the Privacy Protection Authority.

## 9. Automatic evaluation

Answers are checked by an AI system, and it can be wrong. The evaluations and levels are a study aid: they are not a professional assessment and are not passed to employers. If an evaluation looks wrong to you, you can report the question from inside the site or write to us.

## 10. Security

Access to the information is limited to your account and the site's team, and communication with the site is encrypted. No system is completely immune; if an incident affects your information we will act as the law requires.

## 11. Age

The site is meant for people aged {minimum_age} and over.

## 12. Changes to this policy

Every version of this document has a date. If we materially change how the information is used, we will ask for your consent again before the change applies to you.
