# Full-site design simulations

User request: expand Studio, Ion dark, Ion cream/purple, and a switchable light/dark
Ion into complete front-end simulations. A fifth direction adapts the engineering
clarity of Voltage Learning for an Israeli audience without copying its composition.
These are unselected alternatives, not a replacement for the production application.

## Direction contract

THESIS: compare how the same complete learner journey feels in five coherent visual
systems, from entry to deliberate practice and reflection.

OWN-WORLD: Studio inherits the original quiet cream/sage learning desk and Hebrew
serif headings. Ion retains violet-black, amber actions, Heebo and the physical chip.
Ion Light translates that same system into warm cream, deep-purple ink and restrained
violet surfaces. Adaptive Ion uses precisely these two palettes, preserving all local
state on toggle. Signal uses graphite, teal signals and warm yellow actions with an
original split engineering console and Hebrew-first navigation, inspired by the
reference's technical clarity rather than its exact layout or commercial claims.

STORY: home, demonstration sign-in, goal setup, today/plan, searchable library, saved
questions, question workspace, feedback example, text interview, progress/history,
profile and help. Every offered action navigates or changes local demonstration state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Studio's generous editorial desk and XOR experiment; Ion's existing
spatial chip and oversized thesis; light Ion repeats its geometry in cream/purple;
adaptive Ion adds an accessible sun/moon control; Signal pairs a direct interview
preparation headline with a large functional signal path and a compact topic rail.
Working screens use readable quieter surfaces within each selected palette.

FORM: user-pinned extensions of existing directions, not a new direction contest.
Code-led comparison under the existing exploration workflow. Production DESIGN.md
and its sidecar remain unchanged until selection. Reuse the previously generated
Ion image with its embedded prompt provenance; technical diagrams remain semantic.

FINISH: complete front-end behavior, responsive/keyboard verification, one detector
pass, fresh finish review, and documentation of the unselected alternatives.

## State and boundaries

All learner data, company associations, scores, coach messages and history shown here
are labelled examples. No Supabase, practice API, model provider, email, upload service
or account endpoints are called. Local answer drafts, saved questions, selected goals,
image previews and circuit edits exist only in memory and reset on reload. Uploaded
images are read locally with FileReader and are never sent to a server. Code editing
and circuit simulation reuse existing browser-only components. A theme or language
change preserves the current question, draft, hints and local history.

Routes live under /site-preview with style and screen query parameters; the comparison
gallery links all five variants and preserves earlier design collections.
