; A>=0, B>0 and A is divisible by B. C,T,S,U start at zero.
; INC/DEC R and flag-based JNZ label only. DEC zero is forbidden.
; JNZ immediately follows arithmetic; END is fall-through completion.
INC A
DEC A
JNZ OUTER
INC U
JNZ END
OUTER:
COPY_B:
INC T
INC S
DEC B
JNZ COPY_B
RESTORE_B:
INC B
DEC S
JNZ RESTORE_B
SUBTRACT:
DEC A
DEC T
JNZ SUBTRACT
INC C
INC A
DEC A
JNZ OUTER
END:
